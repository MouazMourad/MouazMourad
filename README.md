## Featured project — Aqua Nexus

**Aqua Nexus — The Brain of Your Aquarium**

An intelligent aquarium-management platform for **marine and freshwater aquariums**. Aqua Nexus connects chemistry, livestock, maintenance, equipment, lighting, alerts, history, and tank intelligence — then turns aquarium data into practical actions.

- Marine + Freshwater
- Arabic + English
- Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Tank Health & Next Actions
- Alerts → Actions
- Digital Twin 3D
- Smart Dashboard
- Lighting Intelligence
- Maintenance, reports, history, and more
